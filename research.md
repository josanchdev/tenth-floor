## 4. Why Signal Services Fail (~47% die within 18 months)

1. **No verifiable track record** — cherry-picked screenshots, deleted losses
2. **Over-promising returns** — attracts gamblers who churn after first loss
3. **Going silent during drawdowns** — subscribers need communication most when losing
4. **No educational value** — pure signals with no context are commoditized
5. **Signal overload** — too many signals = confusion (our max-2 avoids this)
6. **No community** — no interaction = no switching cost = high churn
7. **Anonymous operators** — no accountability = lower trust

### What The Tenth Floor already does right

- Transparent outcome tracking (TP/SL/expiry all logged)
- Conservative approach (max 2 signals, no leverage, no shorts)
- Market-price entries (act immediately)
- "No signal IS a signal" philosophy
- Full accountability via SQLite + dashboard

## 7. EU Legal / MiCA — The Risk

### The core question

MiCA defines "advice on crypto-assets" as personalized recommendations considering a client's personal circumstances. Broadcasting the same signal to all subscribers is arguably NOT advice because:
- Same content to everyone (not personalized)
- No individual risk assessment
- Closer to "market analysis" than "personal recommendation"

### The grey zone

- ESMA's copy trading guidance blurs the line
- "Not investment advice" disclaimers alone are NOT sufficient
- Assessed case-by-case by national authorities
- Risk increases as you grow

### Safest framing

- "AI market analysis tool" not "trading signals"
- Never say "buy" — say "our AI identifies this setup"
- Never consider individual circumstances
- Publish track record as "historical AI model output"
- Comprehensive disclaimers (necessary but not sufficient alone)

### Practical reality

Regulators focus on large operators, exchanges, and token issuers. A solo operator with a small Discord is low priority — but not zero risk. A 1-hour lawyer consultation (€200–500) is worth it before scaling beyond a handful of members.
